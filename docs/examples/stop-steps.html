Stop pair production at the LHC: two protons collide and make a stop and an anti-stop

The same production at the parton level: two gluons fuse into a gluon that makes the pair

...or by exchanging a stop between the two gluons (t-channel)

Two-body decay: the stop is heavier than top + neutralino, so it decays to an on-shell top

Three-body decay: too light for an on-shell top, so the top is virtual (t*) and gives b W

Four-body decay: lighter still, so the W is virtual too and gives two fermions

Flavour-changing decay: when nothing else is open, the stop can decay to a charm quark

Decay through a chargino: the stop gives a b quark and a chargino, which gives a W and the neutralino

Decay through the second neutralino: it gives a Z (or a Higgs) and the lightest neutralino

R-parity violating decay: with no neutralino at all, the stop decays straight to two quarks

R-parity violating decay to a b quark and a lepton (the stop then looks like a leptoquark)

pp -> stop pair, each stop -> t neutralino

pp -> stop pair, each stop -> b chargino -> b W neutralino

pp -> stop pair, each stop -> t neutralino2 -> t Z neutralino

pp -> stop pair, one stop -> t neutralino, the other -> b chargino

pp -> stop pair, each stop -> b s through R-parity violation
