# MadGraph gallery

These diagrams come from feynjs's MadGraph plugin. To draw your own, set it up as in
[Setup](../setup/index.md#setup-madgraph).

## A gallery, straight from MadGraph

Every diagram below was generated by MadGraph 3.6 and written by the plugin, then drawn by feynjs,
live, as this page loads: nothing was placed or touched by hand. 26 processes made 2,055 diagrams in
all; every one passes `feynjs check`, and not one has a line crossing another. A selection from
each is shown.

### WZ to three leptons and a neutrino, every diagram

Not only the diagram you would draw: with the full final state, MadGraph finds every way to make it,
the W and Z, a virtual photon in the Z's place, a boson radiated off a lepton, a quark exchanged
in the t-channel. These are all ten from an up quark and an anti-down; the same ten again come
from charm and anti-strange.
